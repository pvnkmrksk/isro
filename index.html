Spot a Satellite

Want to see an ISRO satellite pass overhead? Here's how you can try

🔭

When to Look

The best time to spot satellites is during twilight — just after sunset or before sunrise. The sky is dark but the satellite is still lit by the sun above the horizon.

🌍

LEO Satellites

Low Earth Orbit satellites (like Cartosat, RISAT) move quickly across the sky in 2-5 minutes. They look like bright, steadily moving stars — no blinking!

📡

GEO Satellites

Geostationary satellites (like INSAT, GSAT) appear fixed in the sky. They're much harder to spot with the naked eye — you'd need a telescope and dark skies.
