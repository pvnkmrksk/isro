Spot a Satellite

Want to see an ISRO satellite pass overhead? Here's how

🔭

When to Look

The best time is during twilight — just after sunset or before sunrise. The sky is dark but the satellite is still lit by the sun.

🌍

LEO Satellites

Low Earth Orbit satellites move quickly across the sky in 2-5 minutes. They look like bright, steadily moving stars — no blinking!

📡

GEO Satellites

Geostationary satellites appear fixed in the sky. They're harder to spot — you'd need a telescope and dark skies.
